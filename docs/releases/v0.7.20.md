# v0.7.20

Claude session spend now refreshes during active turns through Claude Code's
local cost-summary control, approximately every 15 seconds. It makes no model
or account-billing request, and failed reads retain the previous observation.

After a native counter reset, the status bar shows the latest reported cost
with a partial-history label instead of remaining fixed at an older historical
subtotal. The tooltip retains that older lower bound separately; overlapping
native counters are never added. Complete results keep their precise values,
and late summaries cannot replace a newer result or a retired process's state.

Validation covers Claude accounting, native controls and session status on
desktop and mobile. Unrelated feature suites, including voice, are excluded.
