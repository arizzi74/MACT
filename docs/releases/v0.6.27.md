# v0.6.27

Fixes English Telegram audio replies being read through Piper's Italian voice.
The voice-note language was detected correctly, but Piper always selected Miro.

- **Piper (Miro / Ryan)** now uses Miro for Italian and Ryan for English.
  Automatic matching follows the incoming voice note's detected language;
  an explicit Italian or English preference still takes precedence.
- Piper prepares both pinned voice models, verifies each inventory separately,
  and keeps one language model resident at a time. Adding the English model
  preserves a valid Italian installation if downloading or checking English
  fails. Both models require about 155 MiB of storage in total.
- Older Italian-only helpers remain usable for Italian. English requires the
  new helper to confirm its language, preventing silent fallback to Miro.
- The Audio panel offers **Check voice files** for an existing enabled Piper
  setup. It downloads the updated signed helper and missing Ryan model, checks
  the selected voices, and preserves the configured engine and language.
- Documentation and model notices distinguish the two voices and their terms.
  Native release checks download and test both voices on Linux and macOS,
  for amd64 and arm64.

Validation includes desktop/mobile Audio settings checks, language-routing and
compatibility regressions, real bilingual synthesis with process reuse and
language switching, and race-instrumented Piper → Opus → Whisper round trips.
The English round trip detected English and reproduced the test phrase exactly.

After updating an existing Piper installation, open **Audio** in the operations
console and select **Check voice files** to prepare the English voice. Merely
opening the panel does not download files or change the selected engine.
